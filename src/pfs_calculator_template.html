What drives this estimate

Bars show each input's contribution to the log relative hazard compared with the average trial patient. Red raises, green lowers the risk of progression.

Research use only. Developed on 1,298 patients from the comparator arms of five phase III trials (Project Data Sphere) with internal–external validation (pooled C-index 0.60, 95% CI 0.57–0.63). Discrimination is modest and calibration varied between trials, so estimates are approximate. Not validated for treatment decisions; patients received chemotherapy-based regimens, and few had HER2-positive disease, so endocrine- or targeted-therapy settings are not represented. “Pretreated” corresponds to the anthracycline- and taxane-pretreated capecitabine-comparator trials; its effect combines prior treatment with regimen differences.
Model: Cox proportional-hazards model with 22 predictors; log-transformed WBC, neutrophils, platelets, creatinine and ALP/AST/bilirubin as multiples of the upper limit of normal; inputs outside the development range are truncated to the 1st/99th percentile. Baseline survival is pooled across trials. Missing entries are replaced by the development-cohort median.
